Frequently Asked Questions

Modern root canal treatment is virtually painless. We use advanced local anesthesia to completely numb the area before beginning. Most patients are surprised by how comfortable the procedure is — many even fall asleep! Post-treatment discomfort is minimal and managed with standard pain relief.

Our professional teeth whitening starts from ₹3,500 for take-home kits and ₹8,000 for in-office laser whitening. The in-office treatment delivers results up to 8 shades brighter in just one session. We offer free consultations to recommend the best option for your smile goals and budget.

Yes! We accept all major insurance providers including Star Health, HDFC ERGO, Bajaj Allianz, and more. Our billing team will handle all documentation and claims on your behalf. We also offer EMI options with 0% interest for treatments above ₹10,000.

We recommend visiting every 6 months for a routine cleaning and check-up. Regular visits allow us to catch issues early — before they become painful or expensive. Patients with gum disease, diabetes, or those undergoing orthodontic treatment may need more frequent visits.

Absolutely! We have dedicated pediatric dentistry specialists and a specially designed child-friendly treatment room. We use positive reinforcement techniques and age-appropriate care to make dental visits fun and fear-free. We recommend the first visit by age 1 or when the first tooth appears.
